# ✨ Storicode — Programming Through Magic

> **“Code Your Magic. Build Your World.”**

**Storicode** transforms programming education into an immersive fantasy adventure. Instead of typing plain syntax on dull screens, learners cast spells, grow enchanted gardens, brew glowing elixirs, navigate baby dragons across floating ruins, break ancient curses, and defeat the Glitch Chimera in the Boss Arena.

## 🌟 The Core Arcane Metaphors

| Programming Concept | Fantasy Metaphor | Interactive Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Variables** | **Magic Bottles / Flasks** | Store values such as names, mana, and potion ingredients in labeled crystal flasks. Change the value to see the potion's color and power shift in real time. |
| **Loops** | **Repeating Enchantments** | Repeat a spell without exhausting your voice to make enchanted flowers bloom, stars appear, or crystals multiply across the meadow. |
| **Functions** | **Reusable Spell Scrolls** | Create a named spell once (`build_bridge()`) and summon its power anywhere to bridge bottomless astral voids. |
| **Debugging** | **Breaking Curses** | Step through instructions, locate faulty logic or out-of-bounds hexes, and shatter the dark curses locking the obsidian dungeon door. |
| **Algorithms** | **Baby Dragon Navigation** | Sequence turns, directional steps, and crystal collection to guide baby dragon Sparky safely across hazardous labyrinths. |

## 🗺️ The Five Arcane Realms & Boss Arena

1. **Realm 1 — Variable Village (The Alchemist Sanctuary)**
   - *Level 1:* The Mana Flask — Assign `mana_flask = 100` to charge the crystal flask.
   - *Level 2:* Potion of Luminescence — Assign `star_dew = 3` and `moon_dust = 2`, then brew the glowing elixir in the animated cauldron.
2. **Realm 2 — Looping Woods (The Grove of Repeating Enchantments)**
   - *Level 1:* The Blooming Path — Use `repeat 6 times: grow_flower()` to bloom 6 lotus blossoms across the stone path.
   - *Level 2:* The Starlight Meadow — Scale your loop to cultivate 10 radiant cyan blossoms.
3. **Realm 3 — Function Tower (The Citadel of Reusable Magic)**
   - *Level 1:* The Bridge of Aether — Define `build_bridge()` with 4 crystal segments, then invoke it to span the bottomless astral canyon.
4. **Realm 4 — Debugging Dungeon (The Labyrinth of Broken Curses)**
   - *Level 1:* The Cursed Runes of Malakar — Identify the buggy out-of-bounds rune target (`#9` does not exist on a 3-rune altar!) and change it to `#3` to shatter the chains.
5. **Realm 5 — Algorithm Kingdom (The Labyrinth of the Baby Dragon)**
   - *Level 1:* Dragon Hatchling Journey — Guide baby dragon Sparky with `MOVE_FORWARD`, `TURN_RIGHT`, and `COLLECT_CRYSTAL` to claim celestial treasures.
   - *Level 2:* The Spiral of Crystals — Plan multi-turn spatial sequences avoiding rocks and fire braziers.
6. **Boss Battle Arena — The Glitch Chimera Overlord**
   - Combine variables (charge 100 Mana Shield), loops (4x Starlight Bolt barrage dealing 60 damage), and functions (`holy_smite()` dealing 40 damage) to conquer the corrupted Overlord!

## 🧪 Key Features

- **Code-to-Magic Engine:** Real-time step-by-step visual execution highlighting each block with glowing golden border.
- **Dual Representation View:** Toggle seamlessly between **Visual Blocks** and **Python Syntax View** (`for step in range(6): grow_flower()`).
- **Web Audio API Synthesizer:** Procedural retro-fantasy audio with wand chimes, bubbling cauldrons, dragon footsteps, crystal pings, curse breaks, and victory fanfare. Zero external asset dependencies.
- **Archmage Lumina AI Mentor:** Progressive 3-tier contextual hints, runtime error diagnostics, and educational conceptual analogies.
- **The Arcane Grimoire:** Full encyclopedia of programming concepts, fantasy metaphors, and copyable Python code snippets.
- **Wizard Profile & Sanctuary:** Choose your Avatar (6 options), House (Ignis, Aether, Flora, Chronos), Wand, and Familiar pet (Sparky, Lumos, Nyx, Sprout).
- **Gamification & Persistence:** Automatic LocalStorage saves, XP progression, level-ups, cosmic gems, and achievement badges.
